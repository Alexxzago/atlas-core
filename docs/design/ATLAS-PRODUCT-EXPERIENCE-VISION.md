# Atlas Product Experience Vision

Atlas is the control room for a company-owned customer assistant. The portal should make an operator confident about three questions: where am I, what is ready, and what should I do next.

## Product Promise

Atlas turns company knowledge into safe, channel-neutral customer assistance. The experience must make readiness and limits legible: knowledge must be published, a Profile must be executable, and a channel must be configured and healthy before it can operate.

## Experience Model

1. Establish context: select Workspace, then Company.
2. Prepare the company: create it, collect and publish Knowledge, configure a Profile.
3. Validate behavior: preview or execute only when server-authorized and ready.
4. Activate channels: configure Web Chat or WhatsApp with explicit prerequisites and operational state.
5. Operate safely: inspect status and resolve actionable problems without exposing credentials, tenant data, or provider internals.

## Quality Bar

The interface is calm, factual, and operational. It favors clear hierarchy, visible status, direct next actions, and useful empty states over decorative density. It never implies that AI has knowledge it does not have, that a channel is live before activation, or that a request succeeded before the server confirms it.

## Audience

Primary users are Workspace members configuring and operating company assistants. They may be technically capable but should not need to understand provider internals, persistence, or Atlas's architectural layers to complete a task.
